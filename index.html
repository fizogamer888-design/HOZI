The Maker

Hi, I'm Marina.

I started making glass bead chains for myself because I wanted simple and pretty jewelry for daily life.

My friends loved the designs and asked where I bought them. That is how HOZI was born!

Today, every single chain is still made by hand, one glass drop at a time, right here in Egypt.

I hope the piece you choose becomes your favorite accessory every day, not just for special events.

— Marina
